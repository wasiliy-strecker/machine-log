Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Machine Log processes and stores your machine and counter data on your own device. No account, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics operated by the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Machine Log mobile app, called MaschinenLog in German. It explains which data the app handles on your device and when data can leave your device.

“Locally” means processing and storage directly on your own device, where you use the app.

Controller and contact

Responsible: Wasiliy Strecker, AppFabrik AI, Ludwigstrasse 23, 86152 Augsburg, Germany.

Privacy and legal contact: contact@appfabrik-ai.de. User support: support@appfabrik-ai.de.

Data handled by the app on your device

  • Machine name, machine number, category, location, manufacturer, model, serial number, commissioning date and notes.
  • Counter label, counter type, unit and counter number. A machine can have multiple counters for pieces, cycles, operating hours or quantities.
  • Counter readings, reading dates and times, time zone information, resets and counter replacements, original values, reasons for changes and correction history.
  • Photos, PDF attachments, document scans, locally generated OCR results and technical integrity hashes.
  • Reminder settings, form drafts, production reports as PDFs and export information.
  • Encrypted backup files created by the user.

Processing on your device and purposes

Machine Log processes machine details, counter readings, photos, text recognition, corrections, reminders, production reports and backups locally on your device. There is no user account, cloud synchronization, server AI, advertising or analytics operated by the app. App content is not transmitted to AppFabrik AI or to an AppFabrik AI server.

The data is used to manage machines with multiple counters, document confirmed readings and production increments, account for backdated readings, resets and counter replacements, retain an auditable correction history, and create local reminders, PDF reports and encrypted backups.

The Android app includes an internet permission for its platform and Google libraries. The standalone app does not require an AppFabrik server. The optional development mode uses a local connection to the Metro development tool and is not part of normal operation of the standalone test version.

Camera, photos and text recognition

The camera opens only after a corresponding user action. Gallery content and PDF files are selected through the system picker. The app receives access to the selected files. Number and text recognition uses a bundled Latin ML Kit model that runs directly on your device. A model download is not required for this recognition. The user must confirm recognized values.

After capture or selection, the app creates a JPEG photo for private app storage with a longest edge of no more than 1920 pixels and quality level 88. Capture and location metadata are not retained. The source file itself is not modified, but it is not archived as an unchanged camera original inside Machine Log.

The optional Google document scanner also processes documents on your device. Its interface, models and other components are downloaded through Google Play services when needed. The Google ML Kit libraries may contact Google servers for updates and compatibility information and send technical performance and utilization metrics to Google. ML Kit does not send input images, text or recognition results to Google servers. Further information is available in the ML Kit privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

SHA-256 integrity hashes refer to the files stored by the app. They support detection of later file changes and are neither an official timestamp nor a guarantee of evidentiary value.

Device features and permissions

Depending on the features used, the app accesses the camera, photo picker, document picker or optional document scanner. Notifications, alarms and system-start events are used only to schedule reading reminders selected by the user and restore them after the device restarts. Android permissions determine whether these features are available.

Camera, save-location and sharing actions are initiated by the user. An external link, such as this privacy policy or the GitHub README, opens the corresponding website in the browser.

Export, sharing and recipients

Data leaves private app storage only when the user saves a production report as a PDF or an encrypted backup to a selected location, or sends it to another app or service through the system share sheet. The user chooses the destination and recipient. From that point, the privacy terms of the selected storage or communication provider also apply.

Machine Log does not sell data or share app content for advertising, analytics or profiling. Technical processing by Google libraries is described in the camera, photos and text recognition section.

Security

The app database, photos, attachments and internally stored PDFs are held in private app storage, protected by the operating system. Automatic Android cloud backups and device transfers are disabled for this data. Backups created manually in the app are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a password selected by the user. Key derivation uses PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600000 iterations. The app does not permanently store that password. Recovery is not possible without the password. Exported PDFs are not encrypted by the backup password.

Backups include saved machines, counters, entries, correction histories, photos and PDFs. Unsaved form drafts are not included in backups.

Retention

Your machine and counter data remains in private app storage on your device until you delete it, clear the app data through device settings, or uninstall the app. Machine Log operates no server on which this app content is additionally retained. Corrections preserve original values, reasons for changes and earlier attachments for as long as the entry remains stored.

PDFs and backups stored outside the app remain at the selected location until you delete them there. This also applies to files shared with other apps or people.

Deletion

  • Deleting an entry removes its data, correction history and single-entry reports stored inside the app. Photos and attachments are removed when no saved entry or draft still references them. Deletion may be rejected if it would make the counter timeline inconsistent.
  • Existing counter and machine reports remain separate documents and can be deleted separately.
  • A counter can be removed only when there are no associated entries, drafts or reports.
  • Deleting a machine removes its locally stored entries, drafts and reports, along with its reminders. Photos and attachments that are no longer needed are also removed.
  • Deleting a production report removes the PDF file stored inside the app.
  • Files stored or shared outside the app must be deleted separately at their respective location.

Because Machine Log has no account and no server-side storage, no account deletion or server-side deletion request is required.

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technical access data, particularly your IP address, to deliver and secure the website. This also happens without a GitHub account. We provide this information page based on our legitimate interest in a secure, publicly accessible privacy policy (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

GitHub receives this data as the hosting provider. Data may also be processed outside the European Economic Area. Information about GitHub's processing, retention, international transfers and safeguards is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We embed no analytics or advertising scripts and no external fonts here. This website receives no machine details, counter data, photos, PDFs or backups from the app. Opening an external link in the app loads the destination website in your browser. Its privacy terms apply.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection, and may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it for the future. Requests concerning data we process may be sent to contact@appfabrik-ai.de. We cannot access app content stored exclusively on your device. You manage and delete it directly in the app.
