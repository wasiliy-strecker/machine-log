Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Machine Log processes and stores your machine and counter data on your own device. No account, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics operated by the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Machine Log mobile app, called MaschinenLog in German. It explains which data the app handles on your device and when data can leave your device.

“Locally” means processing and storage directly on your own device, where you use the app.

Privacy and legal contact: contact@appfabrik-ai.de. User support: support@appfabrik-ai.de.

Data handled by the app on your device

  • Counter label, counter type, unit, machine/counter number and production area.
  • Counter readings, reading dates and times, time zone information, resets and counter replacements, notes, original values, reasons for changes and correction history.
  • Captured or selected counter photos, locally generated OCR results and technical integrity hashes.
  • Reminder settings, form drafts, production reports as PDFs and export information.
  • Encrypted backup files created by the user.
  • Additional machine details, multiple photos and PDF attachments from earlier app versions. This existing data is retained and is not deleted by the simplified workflow.

Processing on your device and purposes

Machine Log processes counter details, readings, photos, text recognition, corrections, reminders, production reports and backups locally on your device. There is no user account, cloud synchronization, server AI, advertising or analytics operated by the app. App content is not transmitted to AppFabrik AI or to an AppFabrik AI server.

Each list item represents one production counter. The data is used to document confirmed readings and production increments, account for backdated readings, resets and counter replacements, retain an auditable correction history, and create local reminders, PDF reports and encrypted backups. Existing machines with multiple counters are displayed as separate counter items. Their saved histories and common legacy reports are retained.

Starting with version 1.0.0, version code 3, the Store variant has neither the internet permission nor the permission to access network state. Its own network connections are therefore blocked. The OCR model is bundled and requires no download. The standalone Dev preview uses the same restriction. Dev Debug retains internet permission for a local connection to the Metro development tool. The app does not require an AppFabrik server. Websites opened deliberately are handled by the external browser.

Camera, photos and text recognition

The camera opens only after a corresponding user action. Gallery content is selected through the system photo picker. The app receives access to the selected file. New readings may be entered without a photo or with one current counter photo. Number and text recognition uses a bundled Latin ML Kit model that runs directly on your device. A model download is not required for this recognition. The user must confirm recognized values.

After capture or selection, the app creates a JPEG photo for private app storage with a longest edge of no more than 1920 pixels and quality level 88. Capture and location metadata are not retained. The source file itself is not modified, but it is not archived as an unchanged camera original inside Machine Log. When a photo is replaced during a correction, the earlier photo remains in the correction history.

According to the general vendor documentation, Google ML Kit libraries may contact Google servers for updates and compatibility information and send technical performance and utilization metrics to Google. These include device make and model, operating system and app versions, per-installation identifiers, latency, image format and resolution, input and output sizes, feature versions, event types and error codes. The identifiers are not intended to uniquely identify a person or physical device. Google uses this data for diagnostics and usage analytics. Transmission uses HTTPS. ML Kit does not send input images, text or recognition results to Google servers. Further information is available in the ML Kit privacy information, the Android SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Removing these permissions limits the Store variant's own network connections. It is not a Google-provided switch for disabling all SDK telemetry. This restriction alone does not establish a guarantee about every internal SDK transmission path through Google Play services.

SHA-256 integrity hashes refer to the files stored by the app. They support detection of later file changes and are neither an official timestamp nor a guarantee of evidentiary value.

Device features and permissions

Depending on the features used, the app accesses the camera, photo picker or file picker for backups. Notifications, alarms and system-start events are used only to schedule reading reminders selected by the user and restore them after the device restarts. Android permissions determine whether these features are available.

Camera, save-location and sharing actions are initiated by the user. An external link, such as this privacy policy or the GitHub README, opens the corresponding website in the browser.

Export, sharing and recipients

Data leaves private app storage only when the user saves a production report as a PDF or an encrypted backup to a selected location, or sends it to another app or service through the system share sheet. The user chooses the destination and recipient. From that point, the privacy terms of the selected storage or communication provider also apply.

Machine Log does not sell data or share app content for advertising, analytics or profiling. Technical processing by Google libraries is described in the camera, photos and text recognition section.

Security

The app database, photos, attachments and internally stored PDFs are held in private app storage, protected by the operating system. Automatic Android cloud backups and device transfers are disabled for this data. Backups created manually in the app are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a password selected by the user. Key derivation uses PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600000 iterations. The app does not permanently store that password. Recovery is not possible without the password. Exported PDFs are not encrypted by the backup password.

Backups include saved machines, counters, entries, correction histories, photos and PDFs. Unsaved form drafts are not included in backups.

Retention

Your machine and counter data remains in private app storage on your device until you delete it, clear the app data through device settings, or uninstall the app. Machine Log operates no server on which this app content is additionally retained. Corrections preserve original values, reasons for changes and earlier attachments for as long as the entry remains stored.

PDFs and backups stored outside the app remain at the selected location until you delete them there. This also applies to files shared with other apps or people.

Deletion

  • Deleting a reading removes its data, correction history and single-entry reports stored inside the app. Photos and legacy attachments are removed when no saved entry or draft still references them. Deletion may be rejected if it would make the counter timeline inconsistent.
  • Existing history reports remain separate documents and can be deleted separately.
  • Deleting a counter removes its readings, drafts, own reports and reminders. Other counters in the same legacy machine are retained. Common legacy machine reports are retained while at least one associated counter remains.
  • Deleting a production report removes the PDF file stored inside the app.
  • Files stored or shared outside the app must be deleted separately at their respective location.

Because Machine Log has no account and no server-side storage, no account deletion or server-side deletion request is required.
